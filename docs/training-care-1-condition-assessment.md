This guide goes with part 1 of the collections care and integrity training videos. A condition check records the state of an item on a given day: an overall rating, what was seen, what should be done and by when, and advice for storing and handling it. Photographs go with the check, and the damage can be marked on them. Each check stays on the record, so its condition can be followed over the years. Allow about 15 minutes.

## Before you start

| What | Notes |
|---|---|
| Account | An editor or administrator. The overview dashboard is for administrators only. |
| Plugin | Condition (ahgConditionPlugin), enabled. |
| Version | Plugins v3.121.172 or later. Before it, the administration dashboard showed 0 checks, photos and annotations whatever was recorded. |
| Photographs | JPEG, PNG, GIF or TIFF, up to the size set under AHG Settings, Condition photos (5 MB on the demonstration site). |

: What you need before you start

## 1. Start a check from the record

Open the record and choose **Condition assessment** in the side menu. The condition page shows the latest condition and the condition history of the record. Choose **New condition check**.

A check is only created when the form is saved. Opening the form and choosing Cancel leaves nothing behind.

## 2. The check

The check date is today and **Checked by** is your user name; change either if the check was done on another day or by someone else. Give the reason for the check, for example a routine survey, a loan or a new acquisition.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Overall condition | Required. Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor or Critical. |
| Completeness | Complete, Incomplete or Fragment, or leave it as Not assessed. |
| Condition description | What you see: the binding, the support, stains, tears, losses, pests. |
| Hazards noted | Anything that is a risk to people or to other items, such as mould. |
| Technical assessment | Materials and how they are failing, where you can tell. |

: The check and the condition

## 3. Treatment

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Recommendations | What should be done, and what should not. |
| Treatment priority | None, Low, Medium, High or Urgent. |
| Next check | When the item should be looked at again. It cannot be before the date of this check. |

: Treatment

## 4. Care recommendations

Advice for the environment, storage, handling, display and packing. Fill in only what applies. Choose **Record condition check**.

The check is saved with a reference made from the date and the record, for example CC-20261110-913568, and opens on its own page. The overall condition and the treatment priority are shown at the top. Use **Edit** to correct a check later.

## 5. Photographs

On the check, choose **Manage photos**. Pick the photo type (before, after, detail, damage, overall or other), add a caption that says what the photograph shows, choose the file and choose **Upload photo**. A thumbnail is made, and the photograph is shown on the check and in the report.

## 6. Mark the damage

Choose the annotate button under a photograph. Pick a tool and draw on the image:

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| Rectangle, ellipse | Around an area of damage. |
| Arrow | To point at a small detail. |
| Freehand | Along a tear or a tide line. |
| Text, marker | A label or a numbered point on the image. |

: Annotation tools

Choose the category (for example damage) before you draw; it sets the label and colour. After you draw a shape you are asked for a note. Choose **Save annotations**. The marks are stored with the photograph and listed beside it, so the next assessor can compare.

## 7. The condition report

Choose **Export report** on the check. The report lists every field, the care advice and the photographs, with the number of photographs and annotations. Print it or save it as PDF from the browser to file it or to send it with a loan.

## 8. History and overview

The condition page of the record lists every check, newest first, with its date, rating, assessor and the start of the description.

Administrators can open **Condition Reports Administration** (`/admin/condition`) for the totals of checks, photographs and annotations, the checks grouped by condition, and the most recent checks across the catalogue.
