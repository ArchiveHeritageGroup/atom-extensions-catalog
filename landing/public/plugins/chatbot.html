Ask the Archive (Chatbot)

Public assistant that answers questions from the published catalogue, citing the records it used. Retrieval is filtered to what the visitor may see; generation goes through the AHG AI gateway only.

A public assistant that answers questions from the published catalogue and links every answer to the records it came from. The plugin is self-contained. It touches no base AtoM file and does not depend on the AHG theme or on ahgAIPlugin. It needs atom-framework v2.18.47 or later (the AI gateway client, SearchAccessFilterService and AhgSettingsService) and ahgCorePlugin, whose fetch wrapper adds the CSRF header.
