IIIF

IIIF for AtoM 2.9 and 2.10. Manifests, collections, annotations, search, authorisation, OCR export and media handling. Nothing in base AtoM is modified. No file under apps/, lib/, vendor/ or config/ is touched, and ProjectConfiguration.class.php stays as upstream ships it.
