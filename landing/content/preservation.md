## What it is for

Know that the files you hold are the files you were given, and keep a record of everything done to them.

- Checksums (SHA-256) for every digital object, and scheduled fixity checks that compare them again.
- Format identification against the PRONOM registry, with a format register that shows which formats are at risk.
- Virus scanning on ingest and on demand.
- A PREMIS event log per object: every check, scan and identification, with outcome and date.
- A scheduler, so checks run on their own and the results are there in the morning.

## Limits

It checks and records the files AtoM holds. Copies kept elsewhere need their own checks, and using it does not by itself make an institution an OAIS-certified repository.
