## What it is for

Show digitised images, books and maps the way researchers expect: deep zoom, page turning and side-by-side comparison, from the record itself. Every digital object gets a IIIF Presentation 3 manifest, so the same images open in any IIIF viewer elsewhere.

- Two viewers on the record page: Mirador for comparing and annotating, OpenSeadragon for fast deep zoom.
- Share a view: zoom to a detail and copy a link that opens the same image at the same place.
- Collections: group images from different records into a curated set with its own manifest.
- A validation dashboard for checking manifests, and an administrators' list of shared views.
