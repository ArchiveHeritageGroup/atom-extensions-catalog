## What it is for

Run a privacy programme inside the catalogue: the registers a data protection officer keeps, next to the records they concern. Built around South Africa's POPIA, with settings for other privacy laws such as GDPR.

- Record of processing activities (ROPA).
- Data subject access requests, with deadlines and outcomes.
- A breach register.
- Consent records.
- A scan of descriptions for personal information, with a person deciding what is redacted before anything changes.

## Limits

It supports the work of meeting privacy obligations; it does not certify compliance, and it does not replace legal advice. The personal-information scan suggests; a person decides.
